
# Illustrative Centrifugal Pump Maintenance Reference

> Demonstration content only. This reference is not manufacturer-verified and
> does not provide approved equipment limits or site maintenance procedures.

## Example equipment: Centrifugal Pump CP-101

### Section M-01: Excessive Vibration
**Observation:** Vibration above the site's verified operating limit requires inspection. No verified limit is supplied in this demonstration reference.

**Possible causes:** Misalignment, bearing wear, looseness, or an imbalance.

**Inspection:** Check mounting bolts, inspect alignment, and review bearing condition according to the approved site procedure.

### Section M-02: High Bearing Temperature
**Observation:** Bearing temperature above the site's verified operating limit requires investigation. No verified limit is supplied in this demonstration reference.

**Possible causes:** Insufficient lubrication, bearing damage, or excessive load.

**Inspection:** Verify the sensor reading, review lubrication history, and inspect the bearing according to the approved maintenance procedure.

### Section M-03: Low Discharge Pressure
**Observation:** Discharge pressure outside the site's verified operating range requires investigation. No verified range is supplied in this demonstration reference.

**Possible causes:** Restricted suction, leakage, blockage, or pump operating conditions.

**Inspection:** Check pressure instrumentation and review suction and discharge conditions using the approved site procedure.

### Section M-04: Maintenance Work Orders
Record the equipment identifier, reported symptoms, operating events, sensor readings, evidence reviewed, proposed inspection actions, priority rationale, and technician decision.

Do not treat suspected causes as confirmed findings without supporting inspection evidence.

Follow site safety procedures and isolate equipment when required by the approved maintenance procedure.
